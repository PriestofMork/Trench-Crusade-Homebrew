How to contribute

Creators: Fork this repository, then add your own PDFs to the community/ folder. Each PDF added there can be linked from this page, making your rules and lore easy to browse.

Keep root files limited to site content only. Use the community/ folder for shared homebrew uploads and the fonts/ and templates/ folders for required assets.
