## 🛠️ How to Host Your Own Homebrew PDF (Step-by-Step)

This repository uses an automated hosting system. Follow these steps to upload your faction or rules and get a permanent sharing link with working internal document hyperlinks:

### 1. **Fork the Repo**
Click the **Fork** button in the top right corner of this page to create your own copy of the project.

### 2. **Upload Your PDF**
Navigate into the `community/` folder in your fork. Click **Add file > Upload files** and drag your PDF into the window.

### 3. **CRITICAL NAME RULE**
Your PDF file name **MUST NOT** contain any blank spaces (e.g., use `my-custom-warband.pdf`, not `my custom warband.pdf`). Spaces break web views.

### 4. **Submit Your Upload**
Commit your changes, then click the green **Contribute > Open Pull Request** button at the top of your fork.

## 🔗 How to Get Your Link Instantly

As soon as you submit your Pull Request, look at the conversation timeline at the bottom of your PR page.

- GitHub will automatically post a comment saying **"Deploy preview ready!"** within 60 seconds.
- Click that preview link, click on your PDF file, and copy that browser URL. You can share that link with the community immediately! The site owner will officially merge it to the main page later, but your link will work right away.
- **To update a file later:** Simply upload a new PDF to your fork''s `community/` folder using the exact same file name to overwrite it. Open a new Pull Request, and your shared links will automatically update to the new version!
